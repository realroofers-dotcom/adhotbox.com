Why this exists

In February the last option for small sites closed

These are the published minimums as they stand today. A site with a couple of thousand readers a month qualifies for almost none of it.

Ezoic was the one that took small sites. Its minimum was a few thousand visits. In February 2026 it raised that to 250,000 monthly users, and the trade press described it as leaving the small-publisher market. Raptive had already been at 100,000 until October 2025.

Which leaves a great many sites with real readers and nowhere to go.

Why we can do it

This is a billboard, not an auction

The minimums are not snobbery. A programmatic network sells each impression into an auction, and an auction needs volume before it can price anything at all. Below some number the revenue will not cover the overhead, so they draw a line.

AdHotBox does not run an auction. An advertiser buys a place — a town, a subject, a set of sites — the way somebody buys a billboard or a page in the church bulletin. There is no bidding to be thin, so there is no floor to defend.

Which is why two thousand readers in one town can be worth something here and worth nothing at Mediavine. It is a different product, not a cheaper version of the same one.

What is different

Four things no other network does

One

You approve each advertiser

Every one appears on your desk. Accept or deny, one click. Nothing runs that you did not agree to.

Two

Your town is the product

An advertiser can buy one town. The big networks target countries; nobody asks a small site where its readers are.

Three

No exclusivity

Keep AdSense, keep anything. Find out which pays better without giving anything up.

Four

No cookies

Text only. Nothing animates, nothing expands, nothing follows a reader anywhere.

Installing it

Four steps

The plugin costs nothing and there is nothing to buy.

  1. Advertise · How this compares · Apply and tell us where your readers are. You get a publisher key.
  2. Install the plugin and paste the key into Settings → AdHotBox.
  3. Put an advertisement wherever you want one — the block, the widget, or the shortcode [adhotbox size="card"].
  4. Open your desk and accept the advertisers you want.

Or without the plugin, on any site at all:

<div data-ad="card"></div>

<script src="https://adhotbox.com/box.js"></script>

Sizes are rail 300×600, card 300×250, inline 728×180 and leader 970×120.

Refused for everybody

What never reaches your desk

Banned across the whole network. No publisher can switch these on, and no amount of money changes it.

Adult or sexual content Gambling and betting Stock promotion and issuer advertising Token sales and crypto offers Payday and high-interest lending Weapons and ammunition Tobacco and vaping Get rich quick, miracle cures, advertisements dressed as news

Beyond that list you may switch off any category you like and never see it again.

Before you install it, the honest part

This network is new and the demand side is small. Mediavine and Raptive arrive with advertisers already signed. We arrive with the ones we have sold and our own.

So a site joining now will not see much at first. Telling you otherwise would be the fastest way to lose you, and there is no exclusivity here precisely so that you do not have to bet anything on us being right.

Install it beside whatever you already run. If it earns, keep it.
