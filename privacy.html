Privacy

What is recorded, what is not, and why. Written to be read rather than to satisfy a lawyer.

Last changed 29 August 2026

The short version

We do not know who your readers are

No cookie is set. No identifier is stored in anybody's browser. No profile is built. Nothing follows a reader from one site to another.

We record which advertisement appeared on which site, in which slot, on what subject, whether it was actually scrolled into view, and whether it was clicked. That is the whole of it.

Advertisers are shown where their advertisement ran and how often. They are never shown anything about a person, because nothing about a person exists to show them.

What arrives and is thrown away

Any request to any server on the internet carries the address it came from and the browser making it. Ours receives those, as every server does. They are not written to any record and are not kept.

What is never collected, by anyone, at any point

  • No cookie, of any kind, for any purpose.
  • No identifier stored in your browser.
  • No fingerprinting of your device or browser.
  • No record of anything you did before or after.
  • No ability to recognise you on another site.
  • No name, no email, no address.
  • Nothing is sold, and there is nothing to sell.

Advertisers cannot buy any of the above from us, because none of it exists.

Images

Every image in an advertisement is uploaded to us and served from adhotbox.com. Advertisers are not permitted to host their own. An image loaded from an advertiser's server would hand them your address and the page you were on — a tracking pixel, whether or not anybody intended one. That is why the option does not exist here.

Publishers

If you carry advertisements

We hold what you gave us: your name, your email, your telephone if you supplied one, your site's address, its subject, where its readers are, and your own estimate of how many there are.

We hold the record of what ran on your site and what it earned, so you can be paid and so the figures can be checked.

The WordPress plugin stores your publisher key in your own site's database and sends nothing from your server. Everything else happens in a reader's browser, as described above.

Ask and we will tell you everything we hold about you. Ask and we will delete it, other than what an accountant requires us to keep.

Advertisers

If you buy a placement

We hold your business name, contact name, email, telephone, website, and what you told us you sell and where.

Your report shows which sites carried the advertisement, how many times it was seen and how many people clicked. It will never show you who they were, and we cannot provide that at any price.

The rest

Plainly

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare's network. Records are kept while a placement runs and for a reasonable period afterwards so that accounts can be settled and disputed.

Who else sees it

Nobody. There are no analytics companies, no data brokers, no advertising exchanges and no third-party networks anywhere in this. That is not a promise about intentions — it is a description of how the thing is built, and it is why there is no third-party script on any page.

Children

This is a service for businesses. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone knowingly.

If this changes
