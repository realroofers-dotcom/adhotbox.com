Targeting

By subject, and by company

A campaign can be set to one property or all of them, to a subject, or to the specific company a reader is looking at.

Installation

Adding a site takes one line

Add the domain to the roster in box.js. Then, on that site, a slot wherever an ad should appear and the script once.

<!-- wherever the ad goes -->
<div data-ad="rail"></div>

<!-- once, before </body> -->
<script src="https://adhotbox.com/box.js"></script>

That is the whole installation. A site that is not on the roster serves nothing at all, so a stray script tag on a page somewhere cannot start showing advertising by accident.

The rules

What will not be accepted

Written down so it can be held against us

  • Nothing from a company any of these sites covers, from its officers, or from anyone paid by it — including investor relations firms and stock promoters. The research is worth nothing if the subject can buy space beside it.
  • No third-party ad networks. A network serves whatever it wants, and would eventually serve an issuer's advertisement on the page listing that issuer's filing. Every placement here is sold directly and the buyer is named on it.
  • Every paid placement is labelled, carries the advertiser's name underneath, and links to this page.
  • No tracking of people. The network counts which campaign was seen on which site, in which shape, on which subject. No cookie, no identifier, nothing that follows anyone anywhere.
  • No advertiser sees a story before it runs, and no advertiser has ever been told what is coming. Buying space buys space.
