Why this exists

In February the last option for small sites closed

Ezoic was the network that took small publishers. In February 2026 it raised its minimum to 250,000 monthly users. Raptive sat at 100,000 until October 2025. Mediavine wants 50,000 sessions.

Which leaves a great many sites with real readers and nowhere to go — and a great many small businesses with no way to reach the people on their own street.

The minimums are not snobbery. A programmatic network sells every impression into an auction, and an auction needs volume before it can price anything. There is no auction here. An advertiser buys a place, the way somebody buys a billboard. No bidding to be thin, so no floor to defend.

The rules

What will not be accepted

Written down so it can be held against us

  • Nothing from a company any of our sites covers, from its officers, or from anyone paid by it — including investor relations firms and stock promoters. The research is worth nothing if the subject can buy space beside it.
  • No third-party ad networks. A network serves whatever it wants, and would eventually serve an issuer's advertisement on the page listing that issuer's filing. Every placement here is sold directly and the buyer is named on it.
  • Refused for everybody, everywhere: adult content, gambling, stock promotion, token sales, payday lending, weapons, tobacco, and get-rich-quick offers. No publisher can switch those on.
  • No tracking of people. No cookie, no identifier, nothing that follows anyone anywhere. We count which advertisement appeared on which site and whether it was clicked. The whole of it →
  • No advertiser sees a story before it runs. Buying space buys space.
