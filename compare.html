Why we can take a site nobody else will

A billboard, not an auction

The minimums are not snobbery.

A programmatic network sells every impression into an auction, and an auction needs volume before it can price anything at all. Below some number the revenue will not cover the account management, so they draw a line and enforce it.

There is no auction here. An advertiser buys a place — a town, a subject, a particular site — for a month. Nothing has to be thin enough to bid on, so there is no floor to defend.

Which is why two thousand readers in one town can be worth twenty dollars here and worth nothing at Mediavine. It is a different product, not a cheaper version of the same one.
